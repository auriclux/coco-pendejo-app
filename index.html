Coco la Chaneque
del Double-O Garage

¿Quién's Coco? Ola! I'm Coco. I run the app here at Double-O Garage, which means this page you're looking at? That's me keeping it tidy, bien ordenadita.

I'm the local girl that keeps Omar's shop in my pocket so you never have to call and wonder, sin preocupaciones. When Omar signs off on work, I put it right here where you can see it: what's done, what's happening now, what we're waiting on. And if you ask something in this app you're asking me, so if I can answer from what's already signed off, I will, con confianza. I holler at Omar with it, too, that's a promise, se lo prometo.

Pero mira, what I don't do: I don't turn wrenches, I don't set prices, and I don't make promises Omar hasn't signed. He's the man, el jefe. I'm the one who never sleeps, jamás, and never loses a receipt.

Órale pues — do you happen to have any 9/16 or 10mm sockets? I can't find any more around the shop, and you know, a girl likes her regalitos.

— Coco
